# 0.3.17 candidate

This candidate brings phone features together under **Settings → Phone**. The
public release remains **0.3.16**, which has booted on the watch and been confirmed
with **KEEP**. **0.3.17 is unpublished. Its local build completed recovery transfer
and receiver validation, then booted successfully and was confirmed with KEEP
on 2026-10-10.** Pairing remains unreliable: the user reported a crash before the
code appeared on the first attempt and incomplete pairing after code entry on
the second.
The user confirmed that 0.3.16 still crashes on Weather → Update → Done after
InfiniLink connects; the candidate's replacement flow needs hardware validation.

- **One phone connection:** tap **CONNECT PHONE** and pair once. Music and Weather
  link to the same Phone screen. **AUTO CONNECT: ON** is saved across confirmed
  reboots; new installations and older settings default to Off. **BLUETOOTH OFF**
  disables reconnecting while keeping the bond. Pairing cancellation and Forget
  Phone also turn auto-connect off.
- **Accurate phone status:** a paired, encrypted connection shows Connected even
  when music controls are not subscribed. Music errors stay on the Music screen.
  **INFO** opens saved-pairing details; Forget is available for a saved bond or
  storage error, and unknown or empty pairing state directs users to CONNECT PHONE.
- **Remembered subscriptions:** a pinned-stack bug skipped saving battery/music
  subscriptions made before first pairing completed. The port now saves them
  after the phone's identity and keys are stored, including when it initially
  connects with a private address. Reconnects retain the existing restoration
  path and pending notifications.
- **Automatic trusted time:** valid Current Time writes from the authenticated,
  encrypted, bonded phone update the clock without a separate ACCEPT tap. The
  transport checks permissions before queuing and consuming time; connection
  changes and stale data invalidate pending writes. Repeated nearly identical
  time writes avoid needless clock edits. Weather, music metadata, and incoming
  notifications use the same phone permissions, including a 16-byte key.
- **Shared Weather and Music:** opening or leaving these screens preserves the
  phone connection. The normal Weather Update/Done session flow is replaced by
  the shared Phone screen. Weather pushes update the cache without disconnecting;
  receipt age remains visible.
- **Settings saves wait for the physical radio:** when a save is due, the phone
  session pauses and waits for the asynchronous disconnect to finish before
  internal flash programming. Auto-connect resumes afterward, including with the
  display asleep. Firmware Update temporarily owns the radio and yields it back
  when leaving its screens. These changes address identified lifecycle hazards;
  they do not establish that the reported on-watch crash is resolved.
- **Existing power policy retained:** reconnect advertising still slows to
  1–1.5-second intervals after the initial fast window. The unused accelerometer
  and heart-rate sensor remain off. No sensor polling or fabricated motion data
  has been added to trigger companion weather updates.

InfiniLink still needs **Developer → Force ANCS off**. It can push weather on
foreground connection and location/app refresh, but its periodic background
weather check depends on motion notifications that goPine does not send. A
connection alone does not guarantee fresh weather. Music support targets Apple
Music; system-wide iPhone media and notifications remain unsupported.

Unmodified Gadgetbridge's PineTime integration rejects weather for firmware
major versions below 1, including goPine 0.3.x. It needs companion capability
detection or explicit goPine support, plus a configured weather provider. The
watch does not impersonate an InfiniTime 1.x release. Time, music, battery, and
supported notification forwarding still depend on companion configuration and
phone permissions. See the [pairing guide and pinned source audit](../pairing.md)
for precise compatibility limits.

Local validation passed: race-enabled Go tests, `go vet`, ten C ASan/UBSan
suites, the 77-object BLE archive build, render checks, and the full BLE OTA build
with enforced resource budgets. The application image is 279,724 bytes; reserved
RAM is 22,644 bytes, with a 42,892-byte heap region and an 8,192-byte task stack.
Selected stack-path checks are regression guards, not runtime peak measurements.
Offline OTA validation also passed for the 209,988-byte package. Local ZIP SHA-256:
`078ce945e7b78d304f0849735616b2a9824e825b7c805865961c566695028270`.

Hardware checks remain pending: reliable InfiniLink pairing and automatic
time, weather and music delivery, reconnect after sleep/out-of-range/reboot,
settings-save and updater handoffs, repeated cancellation, and battery endurance.
The phone remains responsible for connecting and sending fresh local time and
weather; saved auto-connect cannot force a suspended companion app to run.
